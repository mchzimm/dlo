DLO 🐀🐂🐅

We are making tiny pretend animals that live inside the computer. They get hungry, thirsty, hot and cold, and they try to stay alive.

🌍🌳💧

A world that never ends, with grass, trees, water and hills.

🐗🐍🦆

Each animal is its own little program with its own brain.

🍽️💤🏃

They eat, drink, hide, run away or hunt, all by themselves.

📖🔒

Each one keeps a diary of big moments nobody can change.

🖼️👀

A window lets you watch them from above.

📝 write the job on a card→🔨 build it→ 📏 measure it→💥 try to break the check→✅ done
